SolSnipe Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: May 28, 2026

SolSnipe ("we", "us", or "our") operates the SolSnipe mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: email address and password (managed by Clerk, Inc.).
  • Usage data: token watchlists, paper-trade positions, and alert preferences stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase data: subscription status and transaction receipts (managed by RevenueCat, Inc.). We never see raw payment-card details.
  • Device data: device type, OS version, and app version for crash diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • To display your watchlist, alerts, and paper-trade portfolio.
  • To process and verify your SolSnipe Pro subscription.
  • To investigate crashes and improve app stability.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following service providers under strict data-processing agreements:

  • Clerk, Inc. – authentication (clerk.com)
  • RevenueCat, Inc. – subscription management (revenuecat.com)

4. Data Retention

Account and usage data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We follow industry-standard security practices.

6. Children

SolSnipe is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or by email.

8. Contact

Questions? Email us at support@solsnipe.app.
